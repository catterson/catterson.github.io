R Exam - Perceptions of the Wealthy

Description of Dataset and Questions

Description of Dataset. These data come from a study on Perceptions of Inequality and Income in American Households. US participants were recruited online using a survey platform and asked to answer a variety of questions about their personal demographics (e.g., age, gender, Household Income) and attitudes about income inequality in the United States (e.g., how fair or unfair they think household incomes are distributed across the US.)

Questions : You are on a team who wants to make predictions about how much people believe household incomes are fairly distributed in the US (DV = fairness). Define a series of linear models to answer the following questions.

  1. Do people with higher personal incomes (IV = Household_Income) believe that household incomes in are fairly distributed (DV = fairness)?

  2. Are there gender differences (IV = gender) in people’s beliefs about how much household incomes are fairly distributed (DV = fairness)?

  3. What is the correlation between how satisfied a person is about the distribution of household incomes in the US (IV = satisfaction) and how much they believe household incomes are fairly distributed (DV = fairness)?

  4. What is one way you might use these results to try and change people’s attitudes about fairness (e.g., how could you use these results to inform future work that tries to make people believe the US is more fair or less fair?) Make sure to reference the relevant statistics as needed. Note : there are lots of different ways you might answer this question, and there is not necessarily a “right” or “wrong” answer here; we just want to see that you are thinking about the results and how to apply them in the real-world!

  5. AI Disclosure : Please include all prompts that you used for any AI tools. As a reminder, it’s fine to use AI as long as a) you are transparent about doing so and b) you are demonstrating that you are using and applying the skills that we learned this summer (e.g., you are not just feeding the questions into AI and pasting the results.) Reach out in advance if you have questions about this policy!
